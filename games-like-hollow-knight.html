When does Metroid Ravenous come out?

28 January 2027, only on Nintendo Switch 2. It is a new side-scrolling Metroid from MercurySteam, the studio behind Metroid Dread, so it is the closest 2027 game to Hollow Knight on a Nintendo console. See the Metroid Ravenous release date page.

Is there a new Bloodstained game?

Yes. Bloodstained: The Scarlet Engagement, from Koji Igarashi's studio ArtPlay and 505 Games, is coming to PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC in 2027. It was delayed from 2026 in August 2026 and has no month yet.

Is there a co-op game like Hollow Knight?

Blighted, from Guacamelee! studio DrinkBox, is a metroidvania action RPG you can play alone or in co-op. It moved from fall 2026 to 2027 and is coming to Switch 2 and PC.

What is the hardest game like Hollow Knight?

Silksong itself is harder than the first game. After that, Nine Sols and Blasphemous 2 have the toughest bosses. If you want something gentler, Ori and the Will of the Wisps and Animal Well are kinder, and Animal Well has no fights at all. For 3D bosses, see games like Elden Ring.
