Questions players ask

What are the biggest games coming in 2027?

The big names so far include Fable (23 February), Final Fantasy VII Revelation (8 April), God of War: Laufey (20 May), Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis (12 February), Persona 4 Revival (18 February) and Exodus (7 April).

Why is February 2027 so busy?

Many publishers picked February 2027, so Metro 2039, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, Persona 4 Revival, Fable and more all land within a few weeks of each other.

Can release dates change?

Yes. God of War: Laufey already moved from February to May. We mark every game as on time, delayed or scheduled, and update the board when publishers announce changes.

What time do games unlock?

Most console games unlock at midnight local time on release day, but some publishers use one worldwide time. We add exact unlock times as soon as publishers share them.
