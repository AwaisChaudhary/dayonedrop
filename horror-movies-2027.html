When does The Exorcist: Martyrs come out?

12 March 2027 in US cinemas, from Universal and Blumhouse. Mike Flanagan (The Haunting of Hill House) writes and directs, with Scarlett Johansson, Chiwetel Ejiofor, Laurence Fishburne, John Leguizamo and Diane Lane. It is a new story, not a sequel to 2023's The Exorcist: Believer, and it has moved twice: from April 2025 and from March 2026.

When does A Quiet Place Part III come out?

30 July 2027. Paramount moved it from 9 July 2027 so it would not open against James Gunn's Man of Tomorrow. John Krasinski writes and directs again.

Is there a new Conjuring movie in 2027?

Yes. The Conjuring: First Communion opens on 10 September 2027 from Warner Bros. and New Line. It goes back to Ed and Lorraine Warren as young investigators, after 2025's The Conjuring: Last Rites closed the main series.

Is there a new Blair Witch movie?

Yes, an untitled Blair Witch film is dated 24 September 2027 by Lionsgate. Dylan Clark directs a script by Chris Thomas Devlin, and original filmmakers Eduardo Sánchez, Daniel Myrick and Gregg Hale are executive producers.

What horror sequels and remakes come out in 2027?

In a Violent Nature: Part 2 (12 February 2027), The Exorcist: Martyrs (12 March 2027), Wolf Cre3k (19 March 2027), Possession (11 June 2027), A Quiet Place Part III (30 July 2027), The Conjuring: First Communion (10 September 2027) and Blair Witch (untitled) (24 September 2027).
