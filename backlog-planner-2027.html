How do I plan a gaming backlog for 2027?

  1. Star the games you really plan to buy on the departures board.
  2. Be honest about your weekly hours. Two evenings and a weekend afternoon is often 8 to 10 hours.
  3. Put your own hour guess on each game. Long RPGs take far more than a story shooter.
  4. Where the planner shows you falling behind, wait for a sale on the later game or drop one. A date change can also open space, so check the delay tracker.
