Is God of War Laufey coming to PC?

Not announced. Only PS5 is confirmed. God of War (2018) and Ragnarök both came to PC later, but Sony has said nothing about a PC version of Laufey yet. PC players can start with those two, Black Myth: Wukong and Hellblade II.

What should I play before God of War Laufey?

God of War (2018), then God of War Ragnarök. Laufey's story runs alongside both games, and Faye is the mother whose death starts the 2018 game. Ragnarök's free Valhalla mode is a short extra if you have time.

What games are like God of War on Xbox and Switch 2?

Xbox: Black Myth: Wukong, Senua's Saga: Hellblade II, Star Wars Jedi: Survivor, Assassin's Creed Valhalla, Immortals Fenyx Rising and Devil May Cry 5. Switch and Switch 2: Immortals Fenyx Rising and Bayonetta 3. In 2027, Muramasa: Revenant Blades, Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, Wo Long 2: Wings of Ember, Lords of the Fallen II and Kingdom Hearts IV are coming to Switch 2.
